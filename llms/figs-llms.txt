# FIGS

> FIGS, Inc. (NYSE: FIGS) is a founder-led, direct-to-consumer healthcare apparel and lifestyle brand founded in 2013 by Heather Hasson and Trina Spear, headquartered in Santa Monica, California. FIGS designs and sells premium scrubwear and non-scrubwear directly to healthcare professionals. It is a consumer e-commerce and retail brand with no public developer API.
